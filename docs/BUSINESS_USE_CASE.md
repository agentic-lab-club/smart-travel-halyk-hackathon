# Business Use Case

## Core Business Story

Halyk Travel is currently easy to perceive as a ticket purchase service. The business opportunity is to reposition it as a trip management layer that creates more value before, during, and around travel.

The prototype supports that narrative by showing how one trip can expand into:

- transport
- hotel
- attractions
- events
- insurance visibility
- budget and cashback awareness

## Business Objective

Increase transaction density per travel journey by extending the user path beyond ticket purchase.

## Problem for Business

If the journey ends after transport booking, the ecosystem misses:

- additional travel-related actions
- upsell moments
- user retention touchpoints
- stronger habit formation around Halyk Travel

## Proposed Business Model Direction

For the MVP and pitch, the product shows a future path where Halyk can monetize:

- travel attach products
- partner placements
- event-driven travel discovery
- ecosystem retention through cashback and bonus mechanics

## User-to-Business Flow

1. User creates a trip
2. AI gathers intent and budget context
3. User confirms the trip
4. System assembles a travel dashboard
5. User interacts with multiple travel components instead of a single booking step

## Why This Matters for Halyk

- stronger ecosystem positioning
- more reasons to stay inside one superapp flow
- clearer pitch for travel as an activation surface
- opportunity to link planning, conversion, and loyalty in one narrative

## Prototype Constraint

All business mechanics in the current prototype are mock-based. The goal is not to prove live conversion, but to prove:

- product direction
- UX coherence
- architecture readiness
- attach-rate logic
