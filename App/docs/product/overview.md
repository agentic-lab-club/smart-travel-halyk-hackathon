# Product Overview

## Product idea

**Halyk Travel** is a smart travel planner inside the Halyk banking ecosystem.

It creates ready-made trip plans, estimates real budget, considers visa rules, user habits, seasonal events, hotel location, transport cost, cashback and financial challenges.

The user should not configure AI. AI works quietly in the background:
- recommends;
- explains;
- warns;
- sorts;
- simplifies decisions.

## Hackathon angle

The product combines two directions:

1. **Travel planning**
   - ready trip route;
   - transport;
   - hotel;
   - districts;
   - events;
   - attractions.

2. **ML financial challenges**
   - use transaction patterns to personalize travel suggestions;
   - generate cashback and achievable financial quests.

## Core pitch

A bank understands the user’s financial style better than a generic travel app.

So Halyk Travel can recommend not just where to go, but where to go conveniently, realistically and profitably.

## Target users

- Young Halyk users who want to travel without opening 15 tabs.
- Users who care about budget, cashback and clear total cost.
- Spontaneous travelers who ask: “where can I go this weekend / visa-free / during this season?”
- Families or friend groups where preferences must be considered without a long survey.

## Core promise

Not an “AI travel agent”, but a **smart trip shelf**:
- ready options;
- clear reasons;
- real approximate price;
- minimal manual input.

## MVP must-have

- Main screen with AI-powered destination recommendations.
- Recommendation types:
  - similar to previous trips;
  - opposite to previous trips;
  - seasonal/event-based.
- Lightweight filters:
  - visa-free;
  - region;
  - rest type;
  - budget style.
- Selected trip screen:
  - timeline;
  - smart map;
  - cost breakdown;
  - weather;
  - visa;
  - hotel;
  - transport;
  - districts;
  - events;
  - attractions.
- Cashback/reward mechanics.
- Chat/helper only as fallback if generated trips are not good.

## Cut from MVP

- Full booking flow across all providers.
- Complex group collaboration.
- Full real integrations with every travel provider.
- Too many filters.
